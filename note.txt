Perception v4 Ideas
===================

1. Multiple Projections
   - Show multiple views of the volume (XY, XZ, YZ max projections?)
   - Different z-slices or focal planes
   - Give the model more spatial context for classification

2. Separate Perception and Cognition
   - Perception: feature extraction from images (what do I see?)
   - Cognition: reasoning and classification (what does it mean?)
   - Cleaner architecture, potentially more interpretable
   - Could allow different models/approaches for each stage
